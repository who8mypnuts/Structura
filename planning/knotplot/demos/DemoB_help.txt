DemoB Help

It's best to NOT say much about most of demos on this panel.  Except for the
following:

   --- many of the demos rely on certain setting in your graphics driver. try
       the `flicker test' first and, depending on whether you see flickering or
       not, you may have to adjust your settings.

   --- at the bottom are buttons to go into and out of anaglyph stereo mode (that
       cheapo stereo 3D method using red/green or red/blue glasses).  some of the
       demos (knottype, St. Elmo, J, E, Ea, Eb, Ec, the painting demos) do not 
       work in this display mode.  Results may vary.

   --- the demos are tuned for machines that I have access to.  You may find they
       run too fast or too slow on your machine.  An example is the `snakes' 
       demo.  Try changing the values of ncur and nseg (Main panel) to affect the
       speed.  

  --- the `Never ending exhibitions' are just that.  After clicking on these 
       buttons, KnotPlot will go into fullscreen mode and run forever until you 
       press the ESC button.
