Editing with KnotPlot

To direct maniulate a knot, click down in the view window near the bead you want to move and drag in the direction you want it moved. It helps to be in Beads and Sticks mode. You can also rotate the scene as usual with the left mouse and scale with the middle mouse (but you can't translate). Beads are always moved in a direction perpendicular to the line of sight.

  • dmsize changes the 'zone of influence', set to 0 if you want to move only one bead at a time.
  • dmmult is a scaling factor that controls how much beads are moved.
  • dmdecay is the factor by which the movement falls off.

Note that you can display the knot energy while you're manipulating the beads.
